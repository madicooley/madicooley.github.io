Projects

Two engineering collaborations where I used simulation or machine learning to study how design and processing choices affect real materials and devices.

Electrical engineering collaboration

Semiconductor device simulation

How do a semiconductor device’s geometry and materials affect its electrical behavior?

I ran Synopsys device simulations while varying geometry, layer thickness, and material composition. I prepared the simulation outputs as a dataset for planned Bayesian optimization of device configurations.

Chemical engineering collaboration

Materials modeling

How do heating conditions affect the formation and properties of mesophase pitch?

I worked with chemical engineers to apply machine learning to experimental measurements. We studied how pyrolysis conditions and coal rank relate to mesophase pitch formation and the properties of the resulting material.
