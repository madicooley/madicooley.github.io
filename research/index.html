TMLR · October 2026

Localized Operator Learning with Adaptive Partition-of-Unity Mixture-of-Expert Networks

Madison Cooley, Ramansh Sharma, Shandian Zhe, Robert M. Kirby, Varun Shankar

When material properties change sharply across a domain, a single global model can miss important local details.

I led the architecture, training workflow, and experiments for HiRefPOU. Its nested partition-of-unity gates blend local DeepONet experts while preserving continuity. It reduced prediction error against global DeepONet and fixed-partition baselines on heterogeneous Darcy and reaction–diffusion benchmarks. The benefit of adding more levels varied by problem.

SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing · August 2026

Polynomial-Augmented Neural Networks (PANNs) with Weak Orthogonality Constraints for Enhanced Function and PDE Approximation

Madison Cooley, Shandian Zhe, Robert M. Kirby, Varun Shankar

Polynomials are good at representing smooth patterns, and neural networks can help with the parts that are harder to fit.

I helped design and evaluate a hybrid architecture with weak orthogonality constraints between its polynomial and neural components. I implemented custom C++ automatic differentiation rules for the fixed Legendre basis. In our benchmarks, PANNs improved on plain neural networks in regression and for numerical PDE solutions, and beat either component alone on tests with limited smoothness.

TMLR · December 2025

HyResPINNs: A Hybrid Residual Physics-Informed Neural Network Architecture Designed to Balance Expressiveness and Trainability

Madison Cooley, Mike Kirby, Shandian Zhe, Varun Shankar

A model that can represent abrupt changes in a physical field can still be hard to train to find them.

I helped design and test residual blocks that learn how to blend neural and radial basis function components. Our Darcy flow and Allen–Cahn benchmarks show how the hybrid architecture can improve accuracy on problems with sharp or localized features.

TMLR · January 2025

Fourier PINNs: From Strong Boundary Conditions to Adaptive Fourier Bases

Madison Cooley, Varun Shankar, Robert M. Kirby, Shandian Zhe

Neural PDE solvers can smooth away rapid oscillations that matter to the solution.

I helped investigate how enforcing boundary conditions exactly affects frequency recovery, and developed and tested a physics-informed network with an adaptive Fourier basis. Across our experiments, selecting useful modes improved recovery of the high-frequency content.

ICLR · 2024

Solving High Frequency and Multi-Scale PDEs with Gaussian Processes

Shikai Fang, Madison Cooley, Da Long, Shibo Li, Robert M. Kirby, Shandian Zhe

Solving a physical model gets harder when its output varies at several scales at once.

I co-led this study of Gaussian process solvers that learn the dominant frequencies of a PDE solution. We paired spectral mixture kernels with Kronecker structure on grids and showed improved accuracy over neural baselines in high-frequency tests.

IEEE Big Data · 2022

Debiased Learning of Self-Labeled Twitter Data for User Demographic Prediction

Zhen Wang, Madison Cooley, Yang Zhang, Chao Lan, Ji Zhang

People who volunteer demographic information online can differ from the population a model will encounter.

I co-authored this study. We found that FastText trained on self-labeled data generalized poorly on randomly sampled test users and proposed a kernel mean matching approach to address the sampling bias.

SIAM ACDA · 2021

Parameterized algorithms for identifying gene co-expression modules via weighted clique decomposition

Madison Cooley, Casey S. Greene, Davis Issac, Milton Pividori, Blair D. Sullivan

Genes can act together in overlapping groups, which makes it difficult to recover those groups from pairwise measurements.

I led work on modeling those groups as weighted cliques. We proved fixed-parameter tractability in the noise-free setting with respect to the number of groups and tested two algorithms on biologically inspired synthetic data.
